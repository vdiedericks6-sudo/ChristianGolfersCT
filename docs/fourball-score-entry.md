# Fourball score entry and prize-giving review

During an active round, a golfer selects an active member of their fourball, enters that member's gross score (including penalties), putts and other statistics, and saves. The score is stored in Supabase as strokes excluding the separately recorded penalties, so the gross total includes penalties exactly once. Saving can advance to the next player without moving the entire group's shared hole.

Entries made for another golfer are provisional. That golfer must sign in, review and save their own score to confirm it. A playing partner cannot overwrite a confirmed score. On Android, scores are stored in a device queue and retried while the app is running; the round and course must first be loaded with a connection. The sync icon lists pending or blocked entries. A conflicting score requires the golfer to reconnect and explicitly review the server and device values. Pending entries cannot be edited or cancelled until sync resolves. Scores appear to administrators only after syncing. Optional English/Afrikaans voice input requires review before saving and depends on the device's speech recognition service and microphone permission.

In the private admin portal, open **Rounds**, choose the South African play date, and refresh to see database-synced fourballs and cards. The administrator can enter or correct a hole while a round is active, supplying a reason. These entries require the golfer's confirmation and are logged. Export the day's cards as CSV when needed. Missing and provisional scores are marked; completed-round edits are blocked until an audited recalculation workflow exists.

Completion currently requires 18 confirmed scores for every active player. The database does not yet persist a 9-hole round length. Golfers can select their own tee in the lobby before recording a score; the server calculates future results using that tee. Existing historical results are not recalculated. Official competition points remain unavailable until the owner supplies the rules.

Backend migrations `fourball_score_entry`, `require_score_confirmation`, and `per_player_round_tees` must be applied before using this workflow. Rollback-only database checks cover group permissions, tee selection, idempotency, confirmation, version conflicts, audit, completion and completed-card protection. Real authenticated multi-device offline/reconnect, speech recognition and prize-day acceptance testing remain necessary.
